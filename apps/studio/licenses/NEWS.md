BBC RSS fallback

BBC News RSS metadata is copyright the British Broadcasting Corporation.
The application retains publisher attribution, original article links and publication dates.
RSS reuse is subject to https://www.bbc.co.uk/usingthebbc/terms-of-use/#15metadataandrssfeeds.
No BBC articles are fetched or reproduced; only feed headlines are used.
The fallback is limited to the last seven days and the requested topic or a matching BBC category feed.
